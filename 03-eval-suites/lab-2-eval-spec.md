## Part 2 · Three Audience Messages

### A. For Engineering (Jira ticket)
Acceptance Criteria

GIVEN a user query mentions Enterprise pricing and the response contains a dollar amount, WHEN the asserted amount does not match the current verified pricing source, THEN block the response and show: “Live pricing unavailable. Please contact sales for the latest quote.”

Zero tolerance: every pricing assertion must match the verified source. Test against 20 historical pricing queries before release.

### B. For UX / Design
Fallback Behavior

When the pricing guardrail blocks a response, show the user: “Live pricing unavailable. Please contact sales for the latest quote,” with a one-click “Contact Sales” action.

Do not show a technical error or a blank response. Preserve the user’s conversation context so they can continue asking questions.

### C. For Leadership (bi-weekly update)
Business Value

We are enforcing a zero-tolerance pricing-accuracy guardrail for the Ascend IQ assistant to address the fabricated and outdated Enterprise-pricing risk found in the Module 2 audit.

Coverage is strong because a deterministic check compares quoted prices with the verified pricing source. This reduces the risk of incorrect contract discussions, blocked renewals, revenue harm, legal exposure, and loss of customer trust. Results will be tracked weekly.
