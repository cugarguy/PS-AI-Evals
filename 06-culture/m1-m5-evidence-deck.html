03 · Evaluation design

The P0 needs deterministic protection—and the judge still needs work.

Output evaluation

The pricing case was caught by the LLM judge because $49 contradicted the $59 source. The next intended control is a deterministic comparison of every quoted price to the current verified pricing source.

P0 detection logic specified

Trajectory evaluation

A recorded support-agent trajectory scored 1/6 dimensions passed. It skipped the ingestion check and drafted an unverified explanation.

Trajectory verdict: HOLD

Calibration status

The grounding judge measured κ = −0.29 against a required floor of κ ≥ 0.60. That is a failed calibration result, so the judge's output is not sufficient by itself for a release decision. The specified revision rejects claims that cannot be traced to the retrieved source and rejects estimated or rounded numbers.
