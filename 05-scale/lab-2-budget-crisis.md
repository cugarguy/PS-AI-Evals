## Fallback methods (non-Level 3 items)

### L2 · Source Attribution Failure (Robustness · P1)
- **Method:** Weekly compliance-oriented audit of a representative sample of Ascend IQ outputs. Reviewers verify that every material claim, figure, and recommendation is linked to the correct underlying source document or table. Missing, incorrect, or unverifiable citations are logged as failures and added to the regression dataset.
- **Why this fallback is defensible:** Source attribution is a P1 compliance and audit risk, but a human weekly review can assess nuanced source correctness more reliably than a simplistic automated citation check during the initial period. We reserve Level 3 for continuous protection against data fabrication and context failures, while using the audit to identify repeatable attribution patterns for future automation. Any material or repeated misattribution triggers escalation to Level 3 coverage.

### L2 · Data Bias (Fairness · P2)
- **Method:** Weekly stratified audit of sampled summaries across key report segments—such as region, industry, company size, and source type. An SME reviewer compares each summary with its source material to check whether comparable evidence receives comparable prominence, caveats are retained, and the output systematically overrepresents or omits any segment. Findings are tracked by segment and added to the fairness evaluation dataset.
- **Why this fallback is defensible:** Data Bias is a P2 risk that may emerge gradually and requires contextual comparison across report segments. Weekly human review provides a practical early-warning system while the team builds a representative fairness dataset. A demonstrated disparity, expansion into new regions or industries, or repeated client feedback would trigger investment in continuous Level 3 bias monitoring.
